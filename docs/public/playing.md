# Playing

Choose a game on the front page. The player downloads what it needs, starts
the game, and shows it with the N-Gage's keys mapped to your keyboard, your
gamepad or the touch screen.

## What you need

A current desktop browser: Chrome, Edge or Firefox. If something required is
missing, the player says what when it opens.

Phones and tablets work through on-screen controls, but they are much slower
than a computer and some games will not run at full speed on them. Safari has
not been tested.

Sound starts after your first click, tap or key press, because browsers do not
let a page play sound before that.

## Controls

The N-Gage has a direction pad that also presses in (OK), two soft keys under
the screen, a phone keypad and a Clear key. Most games use 5 and 7 as their
main action keys.

Many games have a layout of their own, and the player tells you what each key
does in that game: open **Menu → Guide**. The tables below are the standard
layout, used when a game has none.

### Keyboard

| N-Gage key | Keyboard |
|---|---|
| Direction pad | Arrow keys |
| OK | Enter or Space |
| Left soft key | Q |
| Right soft key | W |
| 0 to 9 | The digit row or the numeric keypad |
| ✱ | - (minus), or \* on the numeric keypad |
| # | =, or / on the numeric keypad |
| Clear | Backspace |

### Gamepad

A gamepad is picked up when you press a button on it.

| N-Gage key | Gamepad |
|---|---|
| Direction pad | D-pad or left stick |
| OK | A |
| Clear | B |
| 5 | X |
| 7 | Y |
| Left soft key | LB or Start |
| Right soft key | RB or Back |
| ✱ | LT |
| # | RT |

Button names are the Xbox ones; on other pads it is the button in the same
position.

### Touch

On a phone or tablet the N-Gage's keys are drawn around the screen: the
direction pad and soft keys on the left, the keypad on the right.

### Changing the keys

**Menu → Controls** lists every N-Gage key with the keys and buttons bound to
it. Add a binding with **+**, remove one by clicking it, and go back to the
defaults with **Reset this layout**. Your changes are remembered in this
browser.

## Saves

Save from inside the game, with the game's own menu, as you would on the
phone. Saves are kept in this browser, on this device.

**Menu → Saves** can export all your saves as one file and import them again.
Use it as a backup, or to move your saves to another browser or device.

Clearing this site's data in the browser deletes your saves, and a private
window keeps nothing after it closes. Export first.

## Display

**Menu → Display** changes how the small N-Gage picture is enlarged: sharp
pixels (the default), smooth, or one of several filters. These change only the
look, not the speed of the game.

## Playing your own copy

The player can also run a game from files of your own. Open **Menu → Load a
game…** and give it a firmware dump of an N-Gage and a dump of a game card,
either as files from your device or as web addresses. Files you choose from
your device are not uploaded anywhere.

## When something goes wrong

- **The player says the browser cannot run the emulator.** It names what is
  missing. Use a current Chrome, Edge or Firefox.
- **No sound.** Click the page or press a key once, and check **Menu → Mute**.
- **The game is slow or stutters.** Close other heavy tabs. The speed depends
  on your processor; the display options make no difference.
- **A game does not start.** Check its label on the front page: not every game
  works yet.
